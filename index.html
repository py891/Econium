About the Olympiad

The Econium Mathematics Olympiad (EMO) is designed to encourage students to explore mathematics beyond the standard school curriculum. The competition emphasizes logical reasoning, mathematical creativity, and real-world problem solving.

Students from different schools compete in a structured olympiad environment that rewards analytical thinking rather than memorization. The aim is to make mathematics more intellectually exciting, competitive, and accessible.

Competition Structure

Junior Division

Grades 7–8

Questions tailored to foundational problem-solving, arithmetic, patterns, reasoning, and introductory algebra and geometry.

Senior Division

Grades 9–10

Questions focused on advanced algebra, number theory, coordinate geometry, probability, and olympiad-style analytical thinking.

Duration
60 Minutes
Questions
25 Problems
Format
MCQ + Short Answer

Topics / Syllabus

Grades 7–8

  • Arithmetic and number theory
  • Ratios, fractions, and percentages
  • Patterns and sequences
  • Logical reasoning
  • Basic algebra
  • Geometry fundamentals
  • Area and perimeter problems

Grades 9–10

  • Algebra and equations
  • Quadratic relationships
  • Coordinate geometry
  • Number theory
  • Probability
  • Combinatorics
  • Sequences and series
