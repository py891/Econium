About Econium

Econium is a student-led academic initiative built around intellectual challenge, mathematical reasoning, and a broader commitment to educational ambition. Through contests, future outreach work, and academic support, the platform aims to create opportunities for students to engage with competitive and analytical learning environments.

The Econium Mathematics Contest 2026 serves as the initiative’s flagship competition, bringing together school students in a structured setting that rewards problem solving, depth of thinking, and academic curiosity.

Competition Structure

Junior Division

Grades 7–8

This paper is designed for younger participants and focuses on strong foundations in arithmetic, logic, introductory algebra, patterns, and geometry-based reasoning.

Senior Division

Grades 9–10

This paper is intended for older participants and emphasizes deeper mathematical analysis, algebraic thinking, number theory, geometry, probability, and contest-style reasoning.

Duration
60 Minutes
Questions
25 Problems
Format
MCQ + Short Answer

Topics / Syllabus

Grades 7–8

  • Arithmetic and number theory
  • Ratios, fractions, and percentages
  • Patterns and sequences
  • Logical reasoning
  • Basic algebra
  • Geometry fundamentals
  • Area and perimeter problems

Grades 9–10

  • Algebra and equations
  • Quadratic relationships
  • Coordinate geometry
  • Number theory
  • Probability
  • Combinatorics
  • Sequences and series
